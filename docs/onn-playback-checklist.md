# onn 4K playback check

Use the remote only. Confirm Settings → About shows the build under test.

- Open Brio. Check that the left rail has visible focus. Move between the rail and the catalog, open a title, then go back.
- Start an episode. Verify center pauses or resumes while video has focus. Check both 30-second buttons, Skip Intro when shown, and Back with controls hidden and visible.
- Open Episodes, switch seasons, and select a different episode. Let one finish and confirm autoplay starts the next episode with autoplay enabled.
- Select another source after playing for at least a minute. Verify the position survives. Try a failing source, then Retry or Choose server.
- Play a video with multiple audio and subtitle tracks. Select a language, open another video with that language, and confirm the preference. Turn subtitles off and repeat.
- Leave partway through an episode. Open Continue Watching and verify the episode number and progress. Try Resume, then Start over.
- Repeat the last two checks on a phone using touch.
